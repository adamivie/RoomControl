◈ HELP / INFO

SIDEBAR
Click nav icons or expand with ☰ to navigate between pages.
COMMANDS
Fire neon buttons for visual effects. Try BURST, RAIN, and WAVE.
FLUIDS
Drag sliders to adjust simulation parameters with smooth GSAP tweens.
SWITCHES
Toggle switches with spring physics. Grab and rotate the dials.
GEOMETRY
Watch the polygon visualizer. Click hex nodes for activation effects.
METRICS
Live-animated progress rings and channel level meters.
WAVEFORM
32-band spectrum analyzer and CSS conic border demonstration.
RADAR
Rotating sweep radar with blips, plus an interactive magnetic field visualizer with N/S poles.
GRAVITY
N-body gravity well simulator. Move mouse to attract particles. Click to collapse. Sliders control turbulence, reach, and drift.
CONTROLS
Rocker switches, toggle button banks with LEDs, and extended toggle arrays for system control.
GEARS
Interlocking gear train with different tooth ratios. Scroll or drag to rotate the drive gear. Each gear maps to a selector value 0–99.
TRACKBALL
Flingable 3D sphere with momentum physics. Click and drag to spin, release to fling. Adjust friction, mass, and sphere size.
ATOM
Interactive atom with multiple electron shells. Grab electrons and fling them into new orbits. Adjust shell count, speed, and fling power.
PIPES
Pipe Dream puzzle game. Place pipe segments to build a path before the flow starts. Score points for each pipe the fluid passes through. Adjust grid size, flow speed, and delay.
COSMOS
Universe expansion/contraction model. Fission reactions cause expansion, fusion reactions cause contraction. Trigger reactions manually or enable auto-mode. Watch the universe scale respond to the balance of nuclear forces.
DNA
Elastic double helix simulation. Two backbone strands spiral around each other connected by base pair bonds. Drag to perturb. Adjust twist rate, elasticity, flow speed and base pair count. Springs restore the helix shape with organic flowing motion.
DEBUG CONSOLE
Toggle with ⌥ button. Logs all interactions, page navigations, and system events.
MOUSE
Neon cursor trail. Hover panels for 3D magnetic tilt. Fluid blobs follow your cursor.
